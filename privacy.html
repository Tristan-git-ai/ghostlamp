Ghostlamp

A cozy daily lantern puzzle

Privacy policy

The short version: Ghostlamp doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app doesn't send anything to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (which nights you've solved, your times, your streak, your bonus rooms), your hints and your settings are saved only on your device, so the game remembers where you were. We never see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Hint packs and the Ghostlamp Pass are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, card or Apple ID. The app only learns which item was bought, so it can unlock it. Apple's own privacy policy covers the payment.

Sharing your result

If you tap Share, the game puts a short line of text (the night, your time and your streak) into your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes; nothing is sent unless you send it. The text ends with a link to this website.

Links

Settings has links to Ghostlamp's TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and to email us. They open in your browser, that app or your mail app, which have their own privacy policies. The game itself still sends nothing.

Children

Ghostlamp doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date below before the change ships, and the App Store listing will say so too.
