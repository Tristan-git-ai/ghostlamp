How to play

Every lantern lights exactly as many tiles as its number, in straight lines up, down, left or right, split any way you like. Light can't overlap or pass through another lantern. Light every tile to chase the ghosts out. Each puzzle has exactly one answer, and you can always reach it by thinking, never by guessing.

On weekends the puzzle has mirrors. A mirror turns light round a corner and the light carries on. Mirrors don't need lighting and don't use up any light, and each one carries just one beam.

Hints

A hint lights one whole lantern for you. You get 3 free hints every day; they refill at midnight. Hint packs never expire, and the Ghostlamp Pass gives unlimited hints and bonus rooms.

Restoring purchases

On a new phone, open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases to get the Ghostlamp Pass back.

Your progress

Progress, stats and achievements are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes them.
