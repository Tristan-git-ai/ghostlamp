About

Ghostlamp is a cozy daily logic puzzle for iPhone. Every night a haunted house goes dark and fills with ghosts, and your lanterns are the only light. Each lantern lights exactly as many tiles as its number, in straight lines up, down, left and right, split however you like. Light every tile and the ghosts flee.

There is one new puzzle every night, the same for everyone in the world. You learn it in a minute, and every puzzle has exactly one answer that you can always reach by thinking, never by guessing. Boards grow through the week, and every weekend mirrors bend the light round corners.

Ghostlamp is free, works offline, has no ads and collects no data. It is made by one person, Tristan Hurd.

Features

  • One new puzzle every night, the same for everyone
  • Every puzzle has exactly one answer, and you never have to guess
  • Boards grow from 5×5 to 8×8 through the week
  • Mirror nights every weekend: mirrors turn the light round corners
  • Five rooms with their own look and sound: the Hall, the Attic, the Library, the Cellar and the Gallery of mirrors
  • Up to three stars on every board. Stars unlock new lanterns and beams of light, which are never sold
  • Streaks, stats, a calendar of your nights and 18 achievements
  • Share your time without spoiling the puzzle
  • 3 free bonus rooms and 3 free hints every day. No lives, no energy timers
